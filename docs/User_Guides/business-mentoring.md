# Business Mentoring

Get expert guidance to accelerate your business growth. Schedule one-on-one sessions with experienced mentors who understand startup and SME challenges.

## How It Works

1. Book a mentoring session through the dashboard
2. Meet with your assigned expert via video call
3. Receive actionable advice and follow-up resources
4. Track progress in your mentorship history

## Session Types

- **Startup Fundraising** - Pitch deck review, investor strategy, financial modeling
- **Growth Strategy** - Market expansion, customer acquisition, revenue optimization
- **Operations** - Process improvement, team scaling, systems setup
- **Financial Planning** - Budgeting, forecasting, cash flow management
- **Product Development** - MVP planning, feature prioritization, user research

## Expert Mentors

Our mentors are experienced founders, operators, and investors who have built and scaled companies. Each brings real-world experience in their specialization areas.
