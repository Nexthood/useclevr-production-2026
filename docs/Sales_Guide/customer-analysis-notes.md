# Customer Analysis Notes

## Target Customers

- Small business owners with recurring spreadsheet analysis.
- Operators who need customer, revenue, product, region, or cost signals from CSV files.
- Accountancy and bookkeeping users who organize receipts, expense coding, reconciliation, monthly close, and tax preparation.

## Discovery Questions

- Which spreadsheets drive weekly business decisions?
- Which metrics take the longest to calculate?
- Which questions repeat after every upload or monthly close?
- Which business profile details change how the data should be interpreted?
- Which support or billing workflows slow the team after analysis?

## Fit Signals

- The customer already exports CSV or Excel files.
- The customer asks recurring operational questions about revenue, costs, products, regions, or customers.
- The customer wants AI answers grounded in their own data.
- The customer values bookkeeping readiness and business context.
