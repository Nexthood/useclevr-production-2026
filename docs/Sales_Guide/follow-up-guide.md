# Follow-Up Guide

## After Demo

- Send the product overview.
- Send the bookkeeping guide when the customer cares about accounting workflows.
- Send a short summary of the customer data questions from discovery.
- List the next dataset or CSV the customer can test.

## Follow-Up Email Shape

```text
Subject: UseClevr demo follow-up

Hi <name>,

Thanks for reviewing UseClevr. The strongest fit we discussed is <customer workflow>.

UseClevr helps your team upload <dataset type>, ask AI questions grounded in that data, and track
the business profile and bookkeeping context that improves the answers.

Suggested next step: test <specific CSV or workflow> and review the first dataset insights together.
```
