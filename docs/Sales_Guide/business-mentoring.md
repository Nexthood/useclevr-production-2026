# Business Mentoring Sales Guide

Positioning and sales materials for the Business Mentoring feature.

## Value Proposition

For founders and SME owners who need expert guidance to scale:

- Skip common mistakes with proven strategies
- Get accountable guidance from experienced operators
- Accelerate growth with targeted mentorship
- Complement data insights with human expertise

## Target Segments

### Pre-Seed/Series-A Founders

- Challenge: Fundraising, pitch deck, investor narrative
- Mentor focus: Financial story, metrics, valuation

### Growing SMEs (10-50 employees)

- Challenge: Scaling operations, systems, hiring
- Mentor focus: Process, team, growth planning

### Established Businesses ($1M-$20M)

- Challenge: Market expansion, optimization, profitability
- Mentor focus: Revenue, margins, strategic decisions
