# Demo Script

## Flow

1. Open the dashboard and show the dataset-first workspace.
2. Upload a sample CSV and show the dataset table.
3. Open AI Assistant, select the dataset, and ask a business question.
4. Show setup progress and explain how account, business, upload, analysis, and page-visit actions improve readiness.
5. Open Business and show profile, location, tax, financial, and review subpages.
6. Open Accountancy and show bookkeeping actions, queue, and monthly close readiness.
7. Open Tickets and show the table-first support queue.

## Closing Message

UseClevr turns uploaded business data into structured dashboards, dataset-aware AI answers, and
operational follow-up workflows.
