# Presentation Guide

## Goal

Show UseClevr as a practical business intelligence dashboard for teams that need answers from their
own uploaded data without building a custom analytics stack.

## Narrative

- Start with the customer problem: spreadsheets contain useful signals, but analysis stays slow and fragmented.
- Show the upload workflow and dataset table.
- Show AI Assistant answering questions from selected dataset context.
- Show business profile, setup progress, and bookkeeping readiness as context that improves analysis quality.
- Show tickets, billing, and account areas as operational support around the analytics workflow.

## Proof Points

- Dataset upload creates structured rows for analysis.
- AI answers stay tied to uploaded dataset context.
- Business profile and setup progress show what context still improves accuracy.
- Accountancy pages organize bookkeeping work around datasets and business details.
- Railway deployment uses generated production output with health checks.
