# Bookkeeping User Guide

The canonical user guide lives at [../../User_Guides/bookkeeping.md](../../User_Guides/bookkeeping.md).
Keep this AI interaction guide aligned with it when bookkeeping instructions change.

## Core Workflows

- Upload bank exports, profit/loss statements, receipts, or accounting exports from Upload.
- Start from the Accountancy empty state when no accountancy data exists yet.
- Upload receipts, invoices, bank exports, PDFs, Excel files, CSV files, or images as accounting documents.
- Review bank reconciliation in the Accountancy overview.
- Review expense coding for supplier costs, payment categories, and tax categories.
- Track monthly close readiness for business profile, financial dataset, and tax context.
- Generate a pre-bookkeeping package after documents are uploaded and structured.
- Export the package as a PDF report, Excel file, or CSV file.
- Send the package to an accountant by adding accountant email, company name, tax period, and notes.
- Use Reporting for dataset-based financial summaries.
- Use Tax for region and business activity context.
- Use Compliance for profile, location, and industry readiness checks.
- Use saved Business Profile context for tax country, currency, fiscal year, VAT or sales tax, payroll, and fixed-cost assumptions.

## Good Data Inputs

- Bank statement exports.
- Card transaction exports.
- Profit and loss statements.
- Sales or revenue reports.
- Supplier expense files.
- Receipt registers.
