# UseClevr Risk Register

| ID    | Risk                                                                         | Cause                                                                           | Impact                                                       | Probability | Severity | Response                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R-001 | Railway test or production deploy returns 502.                               | Runtime startup, environment, database, or generated output issue.              | Users cannot access the app.                                 | Medium      | High     | Validate dist packaging, check logs, review `/api/health`, and keep Railway docs current.                                 |
| R-002 | AI answer appears generic or wrong.                                          | Missing dataset context, weak prompt, provider error, or missing metrics.       | User trust drops.                                            | Medium      | High     | Keep AI answers dataset-scoped, show provider indicators, collect feedback, and use trace learning.                       |
| R-003 | Sales copy overpromises roadmap features.                                    | Future phase items appear in sales docs without a Platform Expansion label.     | Customer expectations become inaccurate.                     | Medium      | Medium   | Review sales material against the phase gates, requirements, and current product behavior.                                |
| R-004 | Tax, legal, insurance, or financing wording sounds like professional advice. | Business Profile and Accountancy features discuss sensitive areas.              | Compliance and trust risk.                                   | Medium      | High     | Label outputs as estimates, user-provided values, or professional-verification items.                                     |
| R-005 | Private data leaks through reports, search, MCP, or FAQ scope.               | Access checks miss a route or tool.                                             | Security and trust issue.                                    | Low         | High     | Keep user-owned data scoped and super-admin access explicit.                                                              |
| R-006 | Stripe checkout or webhook state fails.                                      | Payment provider config, webhook secret, or customer mapping issue.             | Paid conversion and access fail.                             | Medium      | High     | Verify checkout ownership, webhook handling, billing settings, and portal fallback.                                       |
| R-007 | Payload migration replaces core app data.                                    | CMS integration scope becomes too broad.                                        | App architecture and billing/data ownership become unstable. | Low         | Medium   | Keep business and dataset records in application storage; limit Payload ownership to approved content and support issues. |
| R-008 | Documentation becomes confusing for AI agents.                               | Mixed user, developer, sales, prompt, and governance docs.                      | Agents update wrong files.                                   | Medium      | Medium   | Keep folder structure by audience and validate links.                                                                     |
| R-009 | First-use onboarding feels too broad.                                        | Business Profile, datasets, Accountancy, and AI features compete for attention. | Activation slows.                                            | Medium      | Medium   | Use setup progress, guided tour, clear CTAs, and first-upload path.                                                       |
| R-010 | Support volume grows faster than content quality.                            | FAQ and help chat miss common questions.                                        | Tickets increase and users lose confidence.                  | Medium      | Medium   | Review unresolved help chat queries and ticket categories regularly.                                                      |
