# UseClevr Business Case

## Executive Summary

UseClevr creates value by turning business datasets into accessible analysis, AI-supported answers, and downloadable reports for SMEs and startup operators. The commercial model combines free activation, paid plans, Hybrid AI upgrade paths, referral growth, and business-context workflows.

## Problem Statement

- Business owners and operators have useful data but limited time to analyze it.
- Spreadsheet exports rarely become repeatable dashboards or reports.
- AI tools can be too generic when they are not tied to uploaded dataset context.
- Small businesses need decision support before they invest in deeper advisory or BI systems.
- Users need clearer setup context for business profile, accountancy, tax context, and reporting confidence.

## Expected Benefits

| Benefit                   | Description                                                            | Evidence Source                               |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Faster first insight      | Users upload data and ask a first question without SQL.                | Signup, upload, and AI question events.       |
| Better report workflow    | Users download generated reports from analysis.                        | Downloads and report actions.                 |
| Improved business context | Business Profile improves readiness and confidence.                    | Setup progress and review flags.              |
| Reduced support friction  | FAQ, help chat, tickets, and notices guide users.                      | Ticket topics and unresolved chat rate.       |
| Upgrade path              | Pro, Business, Hybrid AI, credits, and referrals support monetization. | Checkout, subscription, and referral metrics. |

## Disbenefits

- Users need to upload data before the product can show meaningful analysis.
- Users may expect regulated advice unless boundaries are clear.
- Early content and FAQ quality affects self-service success.
- Deployment or payment instability directly affects trust.

## Cost Categories

- Product development and maintenance.
- AI provider usage.
- Database and deployment hosting.
- Stripe and payment processing costs.
- Support and documentation maintenance.
- Sales, marketing, and demo preparation.

## Revenue Model

- Free tier supports trial and onboarding.
- Pro Monthly and Pro Annual plans support individual and small-team usage.
- Business Monthly plan supports deeper business workflows.
- Hybrid AI options create additional upgrade value.
- Referral and discount rules support acquisition and retention campaigns.

## Commercial Assumptions

- Users who complete first upload and first AI question are more likely to convert.
- Users who download a report understand the business value faster.
- Business Profile completion increases trust in outputs.
- Accountancy readiness creates recurring use cases.
- Consultants and agencies can become repeat-use or referral channels.

## Measurable Outcomes

| Outcome         | Target Signal                                                           |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Acquisition     | Website visits, signup rate, demo requests, contact submissions.        |
| Activation      | First upload rate, first AI question rate, Business Profile start rate. |
| Conversion      | Checkout review completion, Stripe checkout completion, plan upgrades.  |
| Retention       | Repeat uploads, repeat AI questions, report downloads, history usage.   |
| Support Quality | Ticket resolution, help chat matches, FAQ misses, notice usefulness.    |
| Sales Readiness | Demo completion, objection patterns, sales asset usage.                 |

## Investment Justification

UseClevr focuses on practical SME decision support rather than enterprise BI complexity. The product has a clear path from upload to insight to report to paid plan. Business Profile and Accountancy features create stronger context and recurring business workflows. Super-admin tools support operational control.

Investment follows the phase gates: complete Usable MVP and Sales Validation first, improve AI
differentiation next, and fund platform expansion only after reliability, authorization, retention,
and revenue evidence support it.

## Business Case Controls

- Review conversion and activation metrics after each release.
- Review support tickets and chat misses for content gaps.
- Review pricing and checkout flow after Stripe events.
- Review roadmap claims before sales material updates.
- Keep future features in TODO future until implemented.

## Recommendation

Continue positioning UseClevr as an SME business-intelligence workspace with dataset-aware AI, report outputs, Business Profile context, and guided support.
